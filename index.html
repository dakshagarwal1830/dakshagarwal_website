About

Strategy shaped by the game

I'm a sports strategy and commercial advisory professional who has spent as much time on the pitch as in the boardroom.

Cricket came first. I played competitively through school and beyond, and that is still how I look at sport: from the inside.

I represented Maharashtra at the All-India National Cricket Meet and in AISM Under-19 cricket, played for the Mumbai Mavericks in the Indian Junior Premier League in Dubai, and made the sports pages of DNA and the Times of India for taking 9 wickets in a single innings.

Alongside the game, I built a grounding in its business. I studied sports management at the International Institute of Sports Management, then worked on the ground: running a T20 tournament and its player auction, managing operations for a gaming company, writing sports content and analysing football matches as a performance analyst.

I went on to complete a PGP in Sports Management at Jio Institute, graduating second in my batch. Through consulting with KPMG India's Sports Advisory practice, I've worked on franchise transactions, new sports IPs, multi-year partnership frameworks and growth strategy, including a secondment leading strategy for the International Schools Sports Organization.

How I work

Think like a player

Years of competitive cricket help me understand what athletes, teams, fans and organisers actually need, so a strategy works on the ground as well as on paper.

Build the commercial case

Every idea needs a business behind it: market sizing, competitor benchmarks, rights structures, revenue models and a clear go-to-market plan.

Back it with data

From tagging football matches to studying fantasy sports viewership, I use data to test assumptions and sharpen recommendations.
